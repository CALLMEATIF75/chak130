How to use

Tap a village above to open it, or tap a murabba on the map to see its 25 qilas. Tap a qila for its 8 kanals, a kanal for its 20 marlas.

Use My Location in the field, Coordinates to jump to a lat/lng like Naqsha Zameen, Measure to measure land, and Mark to drop a pin at the map centre. Use My Pins in Tools to save any point on the map.

📖 How to Use
  • Locate: Overview tab → Locate Me.
  • Measure: Tools tab — tap plot corners; area & distance appear live.
  • Search: the search box at the top — name, khata, murabba or qila.
  • Land Records: open the Land Records tab, enter the PIN, then print.
  • Clean map: the 👁 button (top-right) hides everything; Show brings it back.
